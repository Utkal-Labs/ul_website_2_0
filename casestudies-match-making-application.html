Match making Application

Project Overview

Our client envisions the development of a groundbreaking matchmaking app designed to revolutionize the way individuals connect and build meaningful relationships. This cutting-edge digital solution aims to provide users with a unique and personalized matchmaking experience, leveraging advanced algorithms and comprehensive questionnaires to ensure compatibility.

Project feature view


The app has three main features: Events, Matches, Chats
Events: Find out about various community events and gatherings based on different plans. You can see the details of the dates, times, and locations of the events.
Matches: Get matched with other users who share your interests. You can accept or decline the suggestions, check out your existing matches, and request to have meaningful interactions with them.
Chats: Chat with your matches within the community. You can manage your conversations, start new chats, and build personal or collaborative relationships with other users.
