How We Work

From First Brief to Live Product

A delivery process that produces testable output at each stage — so you're not waiting until the end to see if it works.

1. Discover & Architect

Requirements workshop, user story mapping, and tech stack selection — delivered as a written product brief and architecture document. We agree on the build scope, success criteria, and technical constraints before a line of code is written.

2. Build & Iterate

Two-week sprints with working, testable software at every cycle. We integrate QA into the development process — not as a final stage — so defects are caught when they're cheap to fix, not after the feature is shipped.

3. Launch & Maintain

Production deployment, performance optimisation, app store submission, and a post-launch support window. We stay engaged through the first month of live traffic — fixing anything that only becomes visible under real user load.
