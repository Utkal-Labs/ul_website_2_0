Data & Process

How We Work With Sensitive Financial Data

Building for financial services means handling data that regulators, auditors, and your customers trust you with. We treat that trust as an engineering constraint, not a post-launch checklist item.

NDA-First Engagement

We sign before we see. Every project starts with a mutual NDA — not after the first call, not "when things get serious." Day one.

Audit Before We Build

For regulated products, we run a threat-model and data-flow review before writing a line of code. Cheaper to catch compliance gaps in architecture than in production.

Data Residency & Confidentiality

Isolated environments per client, no cross-project data, configurable residency for jurisdictional requirements. Your data stays where it belongs.
