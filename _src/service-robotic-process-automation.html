How We Work

From Opportunity to Live Automation

A structured approach that delivers working bots fast and scales safely.

1. Discover & Prioritise

We run a short process discovery session with your operations team, map candidate workflows, and return a scored automation backlog with estimated time-savings per process.

2. Build & Test

Bot development in fixed sprints with parallel testing in a mirrored environment — so the live system is never the first place a bot runs. Full regression before every promotion to production.

3. Deploy & Operate

Production go-live with a stabilisation window, exception alerts, and a runbook your team can own. We stay engaged through the first month to tune anything that needs it.
