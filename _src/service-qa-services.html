What We Deliver

QA That Covers the Full Test Cycle

We handle every phase — from strategy through sign-off — so nothing slips through.

Test Planning & Strategy

Risk-based test plans aligned to your release schedule and compliance requirements. We define scope, entry/exit criteria, coverage targets, and the right mix of manual and automated testing for your context.

Test Case Development & Execution

Structured test suites built from functional specs and user journeys. We execute across web, mobile, and API layers — with regression coverage updated every sprint so changes don't silently break existing flows.

Defect Tracking & Reporting

Clear defect logging with severity classification, reproduction steps, and linked test evidence. We track defects to closure and provide release-level quality reports that tell engineering and management what they each need to know.

Performance & Load Testing

Stress and load tests that surface bottlenecks before they become production incidents. We model real user patterns, identify threshold limits, and give you actionable tuning recommendations — not just pass/fail numbers.

How We Work

Integrated With Your Dev Cycle

We slot into however you currently work — Agile sprints, release gates, or a one-time audit.

1. Scope & Plan

We review your application, identify high-risk areas, agree on test scope, and stand up tooling — in a single kick-off session with your engineering lead.

2. Execute & Triage

Parallel test execution across environments. Defects are triaged same-day with severity and reproduction steps, so your engineers aren't waiting on information to fix things.

3. Report & Sign Off

A written quality report at every release milestone: coverage achieved, defects found and resolved, known residual risk, and a clear recommendation on go/no-go.
