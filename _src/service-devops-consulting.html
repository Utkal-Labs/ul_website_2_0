What We Deliver

DevOps That Reduces Deployment Risk

From CI/CD pipelines to container orchestration — we build the automation that makes your engineering team faster and your deployments safer.

CI/CD Pipeline Design & Implementation

Automated build, test, and deployment pipelines that reduce the manual friction between a commit and a production release. We design pipelines that catch errors early, run tests in parallel, and gate deployments on quality — so shipping becomes a routine operation, not a risky event.

Cloud Infrastructure & IaC

Infrastructure as Code using Terraform, Ansible, and CloudFormation — so your environments are version-controlled, reproducible, and deployable from a single command. We design for multi-environment consistency across development, staging, and production on AWS, Azure, or GCP.

Containerisation & Orchestration

Docker containerisation and Kubernetes orchestration for applications that need to scale horizontally, deploy reliably, and recover from failure without manual intervention. We move you from pets to cattle — so individual server failures are no longer incidents.

DevSecOps & Observability

Security integrated into the pipeline — not bolted on at release. SAST/DAST scanning, secrets management, container vulnerability scanning, and compliance checks as automated gates. Combined with logging, metrics, and alerting that tell your team what's wrong before users do.

How We Work

From Audit to Automated Delivery

We assess what you have before prescribing anything — then implement incrementally so your team isn't blocked during the transition.

1. Assess & Roadmap

We audit your current processes, tools, workflows, and pain points — then produce a prioritised DevOps roadmap with a clear rationale for each recommendation. You get a written plan you can act on with any team, not a sales pitch for a specific toolchain.

2. Implement & Automate

Pipeline implementation, infrastructure provisioning, container migration, and monitoring setup — delivered in phases that keep your live deployments running throughout. We work alongside your engineering team to transfer knowledge as we build, not just hand over documentation at the end.

3. Monitor & Optimise

Post-implementation review at 30 and 90 days — assessing deployment frequency, change failure rate, mean time to recovery, and pipeline efficiency against the baseline metrics from the audit. We tune what's underperforming and hand off a system your team can maintain independently.
