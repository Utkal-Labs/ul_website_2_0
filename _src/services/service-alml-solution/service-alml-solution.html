Our Approach

A Four-Phase AI Delivery Process

From problem framing to live deployment — each phase gates the next, so you're not committing resources to a solution that doesn't fit the problem.

01

Problem Framing

We start by understanding the business problem — not the AI solution. What decision are you trying to automate or improve? What does success look like in measurable terms? What data do you have, and is it fit for the job? This stage prevents building the right model for the wrong problem.

02

Data Intelligence

Data audit, quality assessment, labelling strategy, and pipeline design. We identify gaps, clean what's there, and define what additional data is required — before any model training starts. This is where most AI projects succeed or fail, and most teams skip it.

03

Model Development

Architecture selection, training, evaluation, and iteration — measured against the success criteria defined in phase one. We use the right model for the problem (not the most impressive one), and we run ablation studies to prove each design decision improves the target metric.

04

Deployment & Monitoring

Production deployment with monitoring, retraining triggers, and a runbook your team can operate. We stay engaged through the first production month to handle distribution shift, integration issues, and anything that only surfaces in live traffic — because production is always different from the test set.
