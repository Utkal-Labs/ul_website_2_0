Real-Time AI Fraud & Transaction Monitoring

Stop sophisticated fraud before it happens, dynamic risk scoring and adaptive friction for every transaction, in under 200ms.

99.97% Detection Rate
< 200ms Decision
1 Problem Statement
False Positives

Legitimate customers are blocked, causing "insult churn" and lost lifetime value.

Account Takeovers

Sophisticated bots mimic human behavior to drain wallets before manual review can begin.

Chargeback Costs

Excessive fraud leads to heavy fines from Visa/Mastercard and potential loss of processing capabilities.

2 Solution
Dynamic Risk Scoring

Every transaction scored 0–100 based on velocity, device fingerprinting, and historical patterns.

Adaptive Friction

Step-up authentication only for "grey area" scores — keeping trusted users uninterrupted.

Velocity Tracking

Detection of card-testing bursts and rapid-fire transfers across linked accounts.

ML Feedback Loop

Chargeback data automatically retrains models against new fraud trends.

3 Key Features
Device Fingerprinting

Unique device signature tracking across sessions and IPs.

Geospatial Analysis

Flags impossible travel scenarios (New York login + Singapore purchase in 10 min).

Behavioral Biometrics

Monitors typing speed, scroll patterns, and touch pressure to detect bots.

Case Manager

High-speed interface for fraud teams to confirm/deny flagged transactions, feeding back into the ML model.

4 Technical Architecture
Ingress Layer

High-performance API Gateway with TLS termination to capture raw packet data.

Stream Processing

Apache Flink / Spark Streaming to analyze data "in flight" without DB writes.

Feature Store

Low-latency Redis/Aerospike cache storing pre-computed risk features.

Inference Engine

TensorFlow/PyTorch sidecar returning a risk score in under 200ms.
