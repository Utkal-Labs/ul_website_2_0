Work Order, Inventory & Invoicing Platform

Project Overview

The project involves testing an AI-powered chatbot developed for a fintech company to provide 24/7 customer support. The chatbot is designed to help customers perform common banking and lending tasks without needing to call customer care or visit a branch. It integrates with the company's backend APIs, CRM, and compliance systems to deliver secure, accurate, and context-aware responses. The QA team's responsibility is to validate the chatbot's functionality, security, performance, and reliability before deployment.
