The Journey of Utkal labs

Starting in 2020, with a mission to transform ideas into reality, the organization embodies the vision of Mr. Nirmal Chandra Hota. Determined to set a benchmark for budding IT companies, he began with just 10 team members. Within two years, the company experienced exponential growth, expanding to over 50 core members dedicated to meeting the needs of a global client base.

Guided by the belief, “We are the products of our past, shaped by present dedication and future aspirations,” the organization thrives under his leadership and continues its journey of innovation and success.
