Work Order, Inventory & Invoicing Platform

Project Overview

The client is an Indian custom-manufacturing business producing made-to-order furniture and upholstered goods for retail and hospitality customers. Production was coordinated through spreadsheets and disconnected trackers, making it difficult to see the live status of jobs, know exactly how much material was on hand versus already committed to a build, or generate an accurate invoice once a job was complete. Utkal Labs designed and built a single web application on the MERN stack that connects the entire operational chain — from job creation to material allocation to final billing — so every team works off the same live data instead of separate spreadsheets.

Project feature view


Work order creation & stage tracking
Live inventory & material reservation
Automated invoice generation from jobs
Guided build configurator with live pricing
Low-stock & material-shortage alerts
Role-based dashboards for owners & staff
